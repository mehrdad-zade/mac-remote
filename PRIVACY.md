# Privacy Policy — Mac Remote

**Last updated: May 21, 2026**

## Overview

Mac Remote lets you control your Mac from your iPhone over your local Wi-Fi network. This policy describes what data the app collects, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

## Data We Collect

### Data stored on your device

Mac Remote stores the following data locally on your iPhone using `UserDefaults`. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party.

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Mac name (the Bonjour service name of your Mac) | To remember previously connected Macs for quick reconnect |
| PIN code | To auto-fill the PIN when reconnecting to a known Mac |
| Last connected timestamp | To sort your saved Macs by recency |

You can delete any saved Mac entry at any time from the connection screen, which permanently removes its name, PIN, and timestamp from local storage.

### Data transmitted during a session

All communication takes place exclusively between your iPhone and your Mac on your local network. No data is routed through any server controlled by us.

During an active connection the following is sent directly to your Mac:

- Mouse pointer movements and click events
- Scroll gestures
- Keyboard input (characters and key codes)
- Control commands (sleep, switch desktop space, disconnect)
- Your PIN (used once at connection time for authentication)

None of this data is logged, stored, or accessible to us at any point.

## Data We Do Not Collect

- We do not collect any personal information
- We do not use analytics, crash reporting, or telemetry of any kind
- We do not have advertising identifiers or tracking
- We do not share any data with third parties
- We do not have user accounts or sign-in

## Permissions

**Local Network access** — required to discover your Mac via Bonjour (mDNS) on your Wi-Fi network. The app cannot function without this permission. No data is sent to the internet; all traffic stays on your local network.

## Children's Privacy

Mac Remote does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a future version of the app, the updated date at the top of this document will reflect that. Material changes will be noted in the App Store release notes.
